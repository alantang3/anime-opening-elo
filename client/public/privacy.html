Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AniTune ("AniTune", "we", "us", or the "Service"), available at ani-tune.com, collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Service. AniTune is a free, non-commercial fan project — an online game where players guess anime from their opening themes. By using AniTune, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture, and your Google account identifier — only the basic profile information Google shares during sign-in. If you play as a guest, we store the nickname you choose and any avatar you upload.
  • Gameplay data. Your rating (Elo), wins, losses, draws, and recent match results, so the game, leaderboards, and matchmaking can function.
  • Uploaded content. Profile avatars you upload, stored with our object-storage provider.
  • Technical data. Your IP address and basic device or browser information, used to operate the Service and to prevent abuse (e.g. rate limiting), plus a session token that keeps you signed in.

2. Camera and microphone

AniTune offers an optional camera/microphone feature so matched players can see and talk to each other during a game. When you enable it, your audio and video are sent using WebRTC — directly between you and your opponent when a peer-to-peer connection is possible, or otherwise relayed through a third-party relay (TURN) server that passes the stream through without storing it. AniTune itself never records, stores, or monitors this stream. It is only active while you choose to turn it on, and you can disable it at any time. You are responsible for what you transmit (see our Terms & Conditions).

3. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and profile.
  • To run matchmaking, gameplay, ratings, and leaderboards.
  • To keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To detect, prevent, and respond to abuse, cheating, or technical issues.

Publicly visible information. Your nickname, rating (Elo), win/loss record, and avatar are shown to other players you are matched with and may appear on public leaderboards. Do not use a nickname or avatar you wish to keep private.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

4. Cookies and local storage

AniTune uses your browser's local storage and/or cookies to keep you signed in (a session token) and to remember preferences such as your volume setting. These are necessary for the Service to work and are not used for advertising.

5. Third-party services

We rely on the following providers to operate AniTune. Your data is shared with them only as needed to run the Service, and each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google — sign-in / authentication.
  • AnimeThemes.moe — anime opening songs and video.
  • MyAnimeList — anime popularity data.
  • Render — application hosting.
  • Supabase — database (accounts, ratings, history).
  • Upstash — temporary in-memory data (matchmaking, sessions cache).
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage and delivery of uploaded avatars.
  • Ko-fi — voluntary donations (if you choose to donate; payments are handled entirely by Ko-fi, not by us).

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and gameplay data while your account exists. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us at the address below. We will delete your account data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited information to comply with the law or prevent abuse.

7. Children

AniTune is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). If you are under that age, please do not use the Service. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International users

AniTune is operated from, and its providers may process data in, the United States and other countries. By using the Service you consent to the processing of your information in those locations.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the revised Policy.
